Deep Dive

The JobNimbus handoff, without duplicates

When an inspector marks a job qualified, one webhook carries the full assessment into GoHighLevel, which creates the matching JobNimbus job. A tag check runs before every send.

  • One payload per assessment. Nothing is retyped or half-filled.
  • Tag guards on every send. Already-handed-off jobs are skipped.
  • 43,000 duplicates removed. The guards keep them from coming back.
